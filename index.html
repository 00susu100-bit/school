Our Educational Programs

Waxaa wehel u ah ardayda manaahij casri ah oo kor u qaadaya aqoontooda iyo heerka garashadooda.

Junior Secondary

U diyaarinta ardayda heerka dhexe iyada oo la siinayo aasaas luqadeed, xisaabeed, iyo maadooyinka sayniska si ay ugu diyaar garoobaan dugsiga sare.

Senior Secondary

Barashada dhameystiran ee maadooyinka STEM, Social Studies, iyo Diinta Islamka si loo baro maaddada heerkeeda ugu sareysa.

National Exam Prep

Barnamijyo gaar ah oo loogu talagalay ardayda fasalka 4aad si ay natiijooyinka ugu fiican uga keenaan imtixaanka shahaadiga ah ee qaranka.
